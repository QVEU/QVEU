"I am a recent graduate of Benemérita Universidad Autónoma de Puebla (BUAP), where I studied biomedicine with a specialization in Microbiology. During my undergraduate studies, my work focused on the phenotypic and bioinformatic analysis of enhancer-binding proteins (EBPs) in Azospirillum baldaniorum Sp245. After graduating, I had the opportunity to join the Dolan Lab as a postbaccalaureate fellow, where my interests center on combining bioinformatic evolutionary analyses with a diverse array of molecular biology techniques to more comprehensively elucidate host-pathogen interactions and contribute to the broader scientific community."
