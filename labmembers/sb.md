"Shruthi Balasubramanian is a recent graduate from the bioinformatics master's program at Georgetown University, where she worked on a range of projects from salmon transcriptomics to big data analyses of JakStat mutations. She originally completed a B.S. in Molecular Biology in her home state at the University of Texas at Dallas. Currently, she works in the Dolan lab as a post-baccalaureate fellow, where she contributes a bioinformatics approach to the study of the evolutionary dynamics of viral vectors."
